title: Emanuele Rossi
description: Emanuele Rossi is a postdoctoral researcher in the Gladia group at Sapienza
  University of Rome, working with Emanuele Rodolà. His research explores how machine
  learning, particularly multimodal models and mechanistic interpretability, can decode
  wild animal communication, shedding light on animal intelligence, consciousness,
  and humanity's relationship with the natural world. His recent work includes OpenWhistle,
  the largest open dataset of dolphin whistles (accepted as a NeurIPS 2026 Spotlight).
  Prior to focusing on bioacoustics and animal communication, he developed generative
  models for structural biology and drug discovery at Vant AI and worked at Fabula
  AI, a deep learning startup for fake news detection later acquired by Twitter. He
  earned his PhD in Graph Neural Networks between Imperial College London and Twitter
  under the supervision of Michael Bronstein, and holds computer science degrees from
  Imperial College London and the University of Cambridge. Outside of research, he
  enjoys scuba diving and hiking.
