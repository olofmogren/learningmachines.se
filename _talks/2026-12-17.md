title: Lester Jame Miranda
description: Lester James V. Miranda is a PhD student at the University of Cambridge’s
  Language Technology Laboratory, working with Anna Korhonen on equitable, multilingual
  natural language processing. His research focuses on building small, specialized,
  and cost-effective language models for edge deployment, particularly for underserved
  communities and Filipino NLP, with publications at top-tier venues including ACL,
  EMNLP, and NAACL. Prior to his PhD, he spent over seven years in industry and research,
  including roles as a predoctoral researcher at the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2),
  an open-source engineer working on spaCy and Prodigy at Explosion, a technology
  consultant in the Philippines, and a bioinformatics researcher studying protein
  function prediction. Outside of research, he enjoys photography and indie game development.
