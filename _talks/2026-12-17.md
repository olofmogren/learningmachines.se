title: 'Lester James Miranda: Language AI for the edge - A data-centric approach'
description: 'The prevailing recipe for building language models is that bigger is
  better: more data, more parameters, and more compute. Although this paradigm has
  produced remarkable frontier models that we use today, there is still a large gap
  in who benefits from them. This is true especially in much of the Global South,
  as Language AI deployment requires models that understand languages beyond English
  and run on small devices with limited connectivity. In this talk, I will discuss
  approaches to building language models for this setting, with a focus on data. Specifically,
  I will focus on improving synthetic data generation for scaling multilingual data.
  Synthetic data has become a common way to scale up training data across languages,
  but it comes with its own problems. I will first present Polyglot Teachers, where
  we propose a metric that combines data quality and downstream model performance,
  and use it to prescribe a recipe for multilingual synthetic data generation. Then,
  I will discuss DataSmith, which combines long-standing approaches to multilingual
  dataset construction with the agentic capabilities of modern language models. Finally,
  I will share preliminary findings on making the data generation pipeline more efficient,
  along with future work on evaluating models on the edge. More broadly, I hope this
  talk offers a different perspective on progress in language models, measured not
  only by what the largest models can do, but by who can actually use them.'
