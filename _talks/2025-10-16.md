title: 'Postdoctoral researcher Lena Stempfle: Interpretable machine learning for
  prediction with missing values at test time'
description: Missing values are inevitable in real-world healthcare data, yet common
  strategies like imputation or missingness indicators often undermine interpretability.
  In this talk, I present methods for building interpretable models that remain reliable
  under test-time missingness. I will introduce Sharing Pattern Submodels, a way to
  leverage missingness patterns while retaining generalization; MINTY, a rule-based
  model that avoids imputation through logical substitutions; and a broader framework
  that adapts trees, sparse linear models, and ensembles to minimize reliance on missing
  features. Supported by insights from a clinician survey, these approaches aim to
  make machine learning both robust to missingness and transparent enough for clinical
  decision support.
