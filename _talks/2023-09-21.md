title: 'Nico Lang: Global vegetation monitoring with probabilistic deep learning'
description: Mapping forest structure on a global scale is an important component
  for understanding the Earth&#x27;s carbon cycle and conserving biodiversity. Several
  new space missions have been developed to support these goals by measuring forest
  structure to estimate biomass and carbon stocks. To analyze the vast amount of remote
  sensing data, efficient modelling approaches are needed that are robust to the inherent
  noise in these data. Data-driven approaches, especially modern deep learning methods,
  promise great potential for interpreting and combining data from different space
  missions to estimate vegetation parameters with enhanced spatial and temporal resolution.
  This talk will present recent research results for global vegetation height mapping
  exploiting ESA&#x27;s Sentinel-2 optical imagery and NASA&#x27;s GEDI full waveform
  LIDAR. By integrating probabilistic deep learning approaches (i.e. deep ensembles),
  the predictive uncertainty of the models is quantified, which is crucial for downstream
  applications that depend on reliable estimates.
