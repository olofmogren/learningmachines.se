title: 'Marcus Liwicki, Frederik Sandin, and Karl Ekström: Technical language supervision'
description: In this presentation we will first give an overview of the recent achievements
  of the machine learning group at LTU and then dive deep into the young field of
  Technical Language (TL) Understanding and Supervision. Recent advances of NLP and
  NLU generated a vast amount of practically useful applications. When it comes to
  technical language with very domain-specific terms, however, the general deep learning
  models are not directly applicable. The terms, abbreviations and TL in general,
  create difficulties, as those do not appear in standard communication. We present
  algorithms to address these challenges and even benefit from typical structures
  in technical environments. The practical use is show-cased on an actual technical
  monitoring systems in process industry.
