description: Hao Wei is a PhD candidate in the Department of Biomedical Engineering
  at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), where he is supervised by Prof. Scott
  Yuan at ABILab. His research focuses on medical image analysis, semi-supervised
  learning, domain generalization, and multimodal foundation models for healthcare.
  Prior to joining CUHK, he earned both his BS and MS in Computer Science and Technology
  from Central South University in China and completed research internships at Shanghai
  United Imaging Intelligence and The Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen).
