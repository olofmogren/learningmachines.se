title: 'Fredrik Lindsten: On uncertainty in machine learning'
description: The rapid development and deployment of machine learning systems is having
  a profound impact on our society. In recent years these systems have excelled in
  a wide range of data-driven prediction problems. However, in many cases, such as
  safety-critical applications, high predictive accuracy is not enough. To make the
  systems robust we also need to reliably reason about the uncertainties in their
  predictions. In this talk I will first discuss the notion of calibration as a fundamental
  requirement for probabilistic predictive models to report reliable uncertainty estimates.
  I will introduce a kernel-based calibration error that can be used to test for calibration
  for general probabilistic models. Second, I will discuss how label noise might influence
  both accuracy and calibration of a probabilistic classifier, and the role of so
  called robust loss functions in this setting.
