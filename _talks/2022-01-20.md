title: 'Jon Nordby: Monitoring noise, machinery and processes using sound and machine
  learning'
description: 'Many physical events and processes create sound, often in the hearable
  spectrum. This makes sound an interesting source of information about such processes.
  By combining sound sensors with machine learning, these can be tracked and analyzed.
  Here we will discuss how we have applied this combination for several different
  usecases, including: Noise Monitoring of shooting ranges and construction sites,
  Process Monitoring of coffeebean cracking during roasting, and Condition Monitoring
  of equipment such as pumps and ventilation. We will discuss some of the techniques
  in use, such as Convolutional and Recurrent Neural Networks, spectrogram pre-processing,
  and edge computing on embedded devices and microcontrollers. We will also discuss
  some of the challenges that come when deploying continious monitoring, such as data
  collection, heavily imbalanced data and preserving privacy.'
