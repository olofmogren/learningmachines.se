title: 'Yuki Miura: AI for urban flood risk and infrastructure resilience'
description: Yuki Miura is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical
  and Aerospace Engineering and the Center for Urban Science + Progress (CUSP) at
  New York University (NYU), where she directs the Climate, Energy, and Risk Analytics
  (CERA) Lab. She also serves on the faculty advisory board of the NYU Stern Volatility
  and Risk Institute and as a member of the New York City Panel on Climate Change
  (NPCC5). Working at the intersection of engineering, climate science, economics,
  and social sciences, her research develops data-driven modeling and optimization
  strategies to assess natural hazards, mitigate urban flood risks, and strengthen
  infrastructure resilience. She earned her PhD from Columbia University and has collaborated
  closely with the National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR) as well as New
  York City and State governments to translate climate risk analytics into actionable
  adaptation policies.
