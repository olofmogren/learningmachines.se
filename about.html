About Learning Machines

A collaborative meeting ground for advanced machine learning practice and research.

The Seminar Series

Learning Machines Seminars operates as an open-access forum exploring contemporary progress in machine learning and applied artificial intelligence. We bring together developers, researchers, and technical practitioners to examine novel methodologies, evaluate real-world trade-offs, and unpack complex implementations.

Each gathering is designed to go beyond high-level overviews, delving into mechanics, computational hurdles, data pipeline strategies, and experimental outcomes.

We mix world-renowned highly accomplished researchers with more junior practitioners to get the full picture. Check out our Archive & Recordings!

Format & Participation

  • Cadence: Held every other Thursday at 15:00 CET.
  • Medium: Presented in English. All presentations include an open live discussion and interactive audience Q&A.
  • Access: Completely free of charge. Sessions are broadcast live online and frequently offer in-person attendance in Sweden.

Intended Audience

Presentations assume comfort with core machine learning concepts and mathematical foundations. The series is particularly relevant for:

  • Academic and industrial researchers exploring model architectures and training dynamics.
  • Machine learning engineers deploying production models to solve complex operational and environmental challenges.
  • Data scientists and software practitioners aiming to stay abreast of verified peer findings and applied methodologies.

Organizer

Learning Machines Seminars is organized and supported by

RISE Research Institutes of Sweden

Sweden’s independent research institute, serving as an innovation accelerator across applied AI, industrial digital transformation, and sustainable infrastructure.

Many of the seminars are co-organized with
